- E2. Both stratum rates lie at a boundary (100% and 0%), so the percentile bootstrap of the protocol has zero
  width. That is not the true precision. Combining the Wilson limits of the two strata (lower limit 85.7% and upper
  limit 13.3%, joint reading) gives 31.0% to 44.7%. The reading of protocol section 8 is the same with these limits.
- A click snaps to the local extremum within 8 ms on the template that the pipeline measures, so part of the
  agreement is structural. r of the PVC equalled the automated value to three decimals in 89% (reader 1) and 80%
  (reader 2) of ECGs. What a reader decides is which deflections exist. Template construction and caliper measurement
  on raw beats were not tested.
- The only ECG-level call differences are two ECGs of one automated-concordant pair. Both changed, so the pair stays
  concordant.
- Form timestamps: reader 1 from 21:45 to 21:57 UTC, median 6 s per ECG; reader 2 from 21:58 to 22:05 UTC, median
  4 s per ECG. The author confirmed that the second reader did not see the first reader's answers. Two SR templates were marked not measurable by reader 1, which removes two pairs from the joint reading.
